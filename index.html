Why test

Aflatoxin is invisible. The losses aren’t.

Aflatoxin is a poison made by moulds on maize and other feed ingredients. You cannot see, smell or taste it. Cows that eat it give less milk and pass it into that milk as aflatoxin M1.

61%

of dairy feed samples from Kenyan feed manufacturers were above the 5 ppb aflatoxin B1 limit in an ILRI study.

Source: ILRI
25%

drop in milk yield has been recorded when dairy feed carries more than 120 ppb of aflatoxin B1.

Source: ILRI

of animal feed samples in records from three major Kenyan labs were above Kenya’s total aflatoxin standard.

Source: Kibugu et al., SLU

Questions

Frequently asked.

What is aflatoxin, and why should I test for it?

Aflatoxin is a poison made by moulds that grow on maize, groundnuts and other feed ingredients, especially when they are stored damp. Animals that eat it grow and produce less, and dairy cows pass it into their milk as aflatoxin M1. You cannot see, smell or taste it, so testing is the only way to know.

How fast will I get my results?

NutriScan results come the same day for samples we receive by 12 noon. Aflatoxin and milk results take 24 to 48 hours. Express aflatoxin results are available the same working day for samples received by 10 am.

How much sample do I need?

500 g of feed for NutriScan, 1 kg for any aflatoxin or mycotoxin test, and 100 ml of milk for aflatoxin M1. Take scoops from several places and mix them, because aflatoxin collects in pockets.

Can you test my milk?

Yes. We test raw and processed milk for aflatoxin M1 and report it against both the East African limit (0.5 µg/l) and the stricter EU limit (0.05 µg/kg). Keep the sample cool and bring it within 24 hours.

Are you accredited?

We are working towards KENAS ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation. Every report states the method used, and results relate only to the sample tested. If a regulator or buyer needs an accredited certificate, we will tell you where to send a split sample.

Who sees my results?

Only you. Results are confidential to the client who submitted the sample. We only ever share combined, anonymised figures, and only with your consent.

How do I pay?

By M-Pesa when your sample is logged. Co-operatives and millers on monthly plans are invoiced monthly.

Do you collect samples?

Yes, within 30 km of Kenol for KES 500 per trip, free when we collect five or more samples together. You can also leave samples at partner agrovets.
